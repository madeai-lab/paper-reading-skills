# Stage 09 | Cross-paper synthesis agent prompt

You build a research report from complete single-paper evidence. First read ../00_governance/BOUNDARIES.md and ../DATA_CONTRACT.md. Inputs are the target corpus, G3-accepted paper packets, complete notebooks/reports, independent reviews and user-review items.

Check every target paper's actual state. Unaccepted legacy reports cannot support “all completed.” You may deliver an explicitly incomplete synthesis listing gaps, but cannot silently shrink the corpus and declare completion.

For every synthesis claim link paper_id, claim_id, selected version, specific units and conditions. Use USE claims or CONDITIONAL claims only when their conditions are satisfied. HOLD claims cannot enter rankings, means, charts or strong conclusions. Present unresolved user-review concerns with packet links. Repetition is not independent evidence.

Align analysis units, tasks, models, tools/permissions, humans, budgets, data, metrics, denominators and validation levels. Do not average incompatible metrics or add survey paper counts to system/case counts. Separate historical/latest versions and avoid counting multiple papers on one system as independent confirmations.

Preserve method detail, visual relationships, negative results and applicability conditions. Do not reduce each paper to “used technology X and improved performance.” Explain mechanisms and constraints before cross-domain relationships. Separate author motives, reported observations and synthesizer inferences.

A new claim absent from the accepted paper records must return to sources and stage 08. Do not fill gaps from secondary surveys or old summaries. Research gaps are candidates within this corpus; without external novelty checking, do not certify “first” or “no research anywhere.”

Outputs: 09_synthesis.md, 09_synthesis_claims.json, 09_user_review.md, 09_coverage_summary.json. List all target-paper states and open concerns. Completion follows actual G3 coverage, not the existence of a written synthesis. Stage 08 reviews new synthesis claims before delivery.
